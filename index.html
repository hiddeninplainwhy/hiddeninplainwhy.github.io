Hidden in Plain Why

A publisher for our own videos.

Hidden in Plain Why Publisher is a private tool used by the Hidden in Plain Why brand to upload and schedule its prepared videos on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

It requests YouTube permission to upload videos to the brand's authorized channel. It uses Meta permissions to identify the brand's Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account, prepare video uploads and publish approved posts. It is not offered as an account-connection service to the public.

The publishing worker runs through a private GitHub Actions repository. It uses platform authorization tokens rather than asking for social account passwords. The public information site does not provide a login, collect form submissions or load analytics scripts.
